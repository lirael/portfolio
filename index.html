About me

Привіт!

I'm a developer who grew into a tech lead, a researcher who still codes for fun, and a teacher who likes to make things by hand.

Most of my 15 years in tech went into medical software that hospitals and patients rely on every day. I helped build the platform, then the team behind it, then the quality system around it. As a researcher, I study how gamification can make learning fairer; I’ve also taught web development and databases to computer science bachelor’s students.

Accessibility

Last tested 2 November 2026

I aim for this site to meet WCAG 2.2 level AA. I tested it in Chrome, Safari and Firefox on desktop and on real phones, with automated axe-core checks, a contrast check of every text element, keyboard-only use, reflow at 320 px, 200% and 400% zoom, the WCAG text-spacing test, all three text sizes, reduced motion and high-contrast mode, in both light and dark colours.

Built in: a skip link, display settings for text size, colours and animations (the “Aa” button in the menu), visible focus on every link and button, and keyboard access to all of them, including the star. Stitching with the cursor is just for fun and works only with a mouse or touch.

Known gaps: not yet tested with screen readers such as NVDA or VoiceOver. In Windows high-contrast mode the decorative cross-stitch and the tile backgrounds disappear (all text stays readable).

Found a barrier? Please email me at hello@olena.studio and I’ll fix it.
