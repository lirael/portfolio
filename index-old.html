About me

Привіт!

I'm a developer who grew into a tech lead, a researcher who still codes for fun, and a teacher who likes to make things by hand.

Most of my 15 years in tech went into medical software that hospitals and patients rely on every day: I helped build the platform, and built the team behind it and the quality system around it. On the research side, I study how games and gamification can make learning fairer, and I've taught web and database courses at university.

Accessibility

Last tested 16 October 2026

I aim for this site to meet WCAG 2.2 level AA. I tested it in Chromium with automated axe-core checks, keyboard-only use, reflow at 320 px, 200% zoom, the WCAG text-spacing test, reduced motion and high-contrast mode, in both light and dark colours.

Built in: a skip link, display settings for text size, colours and animations (the “Aa” button in the menu), visible focus everywhere, and keyboard access to everything, including the stitching star.

Known gaps: not yet tested with screen readers such as NVDA or VoiceOver. The “Copied” confirmation isn’t announced, language levels are read out twice, and the decorative cross-stitch disappears in Windows high-contrast mode.

Found a barrier? Please email me at pastushenko.olena@gmail.com and I’ll fix it.
